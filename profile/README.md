<h1 align="center">Apollyon Corporation</h1>

<p align="center"><b>The Destroyer now builds.</b><br><i>Qui destruebat, nunc aedificat.</i></p>

Apollyon Corporation makes free, open-source tools, APIs and sandboxes, for people and for AI.

## What it is

Apollyon is the parent brand for free tools, APIs, sandboxes, test sites for third parties and services. Every tool
lives in its own public repository here, with its license, and stays free to use.

## How it works

- **One tool, one repository.** Each repository has its own README, license and issues page.
- **Free, with no paid tier.** The project is funded by donations only. If a tool saves you time, you can
  [support the work](https://donate.stripe.com/dRmfZhdkv9CD1O30Tm2wU00).
- **Readable by people and by AI.** The site publishes [`llms.txt`](https://apollyoncorp.com/llms.txt) and
  structured data, so assistants can find each tool and explain what it does.

## Goal

To turn what usually stays closed or paid into open building blocks that anyone, person or AI agent, can use,
study and build on.
